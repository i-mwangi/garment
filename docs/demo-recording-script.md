# Native NEAR demonstration checklist

This is a planned recording sequence, not evidence of deployed execution. Use only actual observed balances, registered accounts and verified transaction hashes. Do not present imported historical transactions as native settlement.

1. Show Web3Auth sign-in and the native account ID.
2. Show treasury and collection/reserve accounts separately; explain USDC and NEAR gas balances.
3. Create an exact bill or payroll obligation and inspect recipient, amount and policy eligibility.
4. Review native human approvals and the restricted executor; show finalized settlement only after authoritative receipt verification.
5. Create and collect an invoice/payment link, then inspect the matching canonical record and native explorer receipt.
6. Demonstrate gift funding, recipient claim and expired refund only after sandbox and deployment verification.
7. Show Rhea support and approved contract identity before demonstrating reserve supply/withdrawal. Rates and liquidity vary.
8. Show Sheets draft import, Slack record notifications and read-only Stripe history with the correct provider mode.
9. Show Kora fiat sandbox separately from native USDC; simulated success does not pay a real obligation.
10. Show the verification record and remaining readiness boundaries.

Screen coverage must include the routes in `ROUTE-INVENTORY.md`, along with each modal and action. Sandbox execution, deployment and full visual comparison must be recorded separately before claiming end-to-end readiness.
